✦Starthread

Draw every constellation in one stroke.

A calm line-drawing puzzle on an old star atlas. Each chart is a constellation of stars and faint links: put your finger on a star and trace every link exactly once, without lifting a single line twice. Draw it and the constellation lights up, revealing its name and a one-line myth for your album. A new chart every night, 60 to collect, no ads.

How to play

  • Drag from star to star. Your finger snaps to the nearest star, so you never have to be exact.
  • Trace every link exactly once. Drag back along the last link (or tap Undo) to take it back.
  • Stuck? A stroke can only begin and end on stars with an odd number of links.
  • Gold pole stars mark where the stroke must begin. Crossing links don't meet — go straight over.
  • Red comet links fly one way only. Twin links are traced twice, on two different visits.
  • One clean stroke (no undo, no hint) makes a constellation shine three times as bright.
  • Tonight's chart is the same for everyone. Draw it every night to grow your streak, and share the result without giving the drawing away.

Help

Hints. Everyone gets three hints a night. A hint shows the next link — and if your stroke can no longer be finished, it first rewinds to where it still can. Every chart can be drawn; the app proves it for each one.

Restore Pro on a new iPhone. Settings › Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account. Pro is a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing.

Refunds. Apple handles payments and refunds: request a refund from Apple.

Move or back up your progress. Everything lives only on your iPhone and is included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup. There is no account or server to restore it from.
